How the numbers work. Each category has 96 fictional accounts sized from public benchmarks. Cost to serve = tokens per task × model price + tool and infrastructure cost. Per-action credits are $0.10 per agent action; with the customer's own model key the vendor charges a $0.03 platform fee per action. Outcome revenue counts only verified, undisputed outcomes. Hybrid = the larger of the platform fee and outcome charges, capped. Accounts with missing outcome signals are excluded from totals and shown as unknown. Ask-the-pane answers are rule-based, not a live model. Accounts, outcome events, recommendations and approvals come from the MonetizeIQ API.
